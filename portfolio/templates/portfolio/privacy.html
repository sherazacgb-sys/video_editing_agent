Privacy notice

The short version. This is a personal, non-commercial site run by one person for experimentation and learning. The Video Editing Agent is a research demo, not a product.

Please do not upload anything personal or confidential: no videos of yourself or other people you can identify, no private documents, nothing you would not be happy to see on a public test server. Uploaded audio and text are sent to AI providers in the United States and China (details below).

1. Who I am

This site (sherazlabs.co.uk) is run by Sheraz Ali, an individual based in London, United Kingdom. I am the "controller" of the personal data described here. I am not a company and I do not trade through this site.

Contact for anything privacy-related: sherazacgb@gmail.com

2. Who this site is for

The site and the demo are intended for people aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, please do not use the demo or send me any information.

3. What I collect and why

If you only read the homepage

  • I do not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools, and the homepage sets no cookies.
  • Like any web server, mine records basic request logs (IP address, time, page requested, browser type) to keep the site running and secure.
  • Your browser loads fonts and styling code from Google Fonts, the Tailwind CSS CDN and jsDelivr. Those providers receive your IP address when it does so, under their own privacy policies.

If you use the Video Editing Agent demo

I do not use your data for marketing, I do not sell it, and I do not use it to make decisions about you. The AI only processes the content you give it to produce the video you asked for.

4. Lawful basis

I rely on legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): running and securing a free experimental demo, understanding how it is used, and improving it. The data involved is limited, and you choose what to upload. You can object at any time (see section 9).

6. International transfers

Using the demo sends data to the United States (Groq) and China (DeepSeek). China is not covered by a UK "adequacy" decision, and I have no additional contractual safeguards in place with DeepSeek, so data sent there may not be protected to the standard you would get in the UK. That is the main reason I ask you not to upload anything personal. If you are not comfortable with this, please don't use the demo; the rest of the site sends nothing to these providers.

8. Cookies and browser storage

I only use what is strictly necessary for the demo to work, so no consent is needed for these. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

  • guest_id: identifies your guest session so you can see your own jobs (30 days).
  • sessionid and csrftoken: standard Django cookies that keep you signed in and protect forms from forgery.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps small settings on your device only: light/dark theme, your cookie-banner choice, which chat you were in, and whether you have already been asked for feedback. These are never sent to the server.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask me to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access)
  • correct it (rectification)
  • delete it (erasure)
  • limit how I use it (restriction)
  • stop using it (objection)
  • send it to you in a reusable format (portability)

Email sherazacgb@gmail.com. Because guests have no account, please include roughly when you used the demo and the name of the file you uploaded so I can find your data. I will reply within one month. Most guest data is deleted automatically within 30 days anyway.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, please contact me first. You also have the right to complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes

If what the site collects changes (for example, if I add visitor analytics), I will update this notice and the date at the top.
