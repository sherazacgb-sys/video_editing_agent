What it does

Six things you can ask for today.

Edit by chatting

Describe the change in plain English. No timeline, no keyframes, no menus to hunt through.

Captions, synced and styled

Whisper transcribes the audio, the agent styles the captions and syncs them to speech.

Image and PDF overlays

Drop a logo, a diagram or a page from a PDF onto the frame at whatever timestamp you name.

One-click Skills

The common jobs are buttons too, for the times you'd rather not type the request out.

No account required

Guests get the whole editing flow. Your video is held for six hours, then cleared automatically.

It knows its own interface

Ask where a control is and it reads the real map of the app instead of guessing at an answer.

How it works

Upload to finished file, in three passes.

PASS 01 · TRANSCRIBE

Transcribed

Whisper turns the audio track into a clean, timestamped transcript of what was said.

PASS 02 · CAPTION

Captioned

The agent styles and places the captions from a plain-English request, then any overlays you asked for.

PASS 03 · RENDER

Rendered

Everything is composited back into one video file, ready to play or download.
